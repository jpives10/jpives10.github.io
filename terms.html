Terms of use

FLL VMS Collector Personal is operated by Justin Ivey for authorized Arizona FIRST LEGO League volunteer administration.

Authorized operation

Use of the collector is limited to the operator's approved volunteer coordination workflow. Access to FIRST VMS and Google Drive must be granted through their normal authentication and permission processes. Their applicable service terms govern use of those services.

Reports and verification

The configured daily collection covers 12 active events and three report types per event. A collection is ready for downstream processing when all 36 reports and their publication evidence pass validation and the completion marker is verified.

The operator is responsible for reviewing reported failures, maintaining authorized access, and allowing downstream processing only after the collection's required validation checks succeed.

Data handling

Use volunteer information solely for authorized program administration and handle it according to the program's permissions and record-handling practices. The application's data-handling details are described in its privacy policy.
