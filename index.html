Volunteer report collection for Arizona FLL

A local application operated by Justin Ivey to collect and verify FIRST Volunteer Management System reports for Arizona FIRST LEGO League volunteer administration.

What the application does

The collector runs on the operator's Mac. It exports Assigned Volunteers, Volunteer Application Status, and Years of Service and Alumni Status for the 12 authorized active events.

It validates each report's event identity, structure, and SHA-256 checksum. A daily batch contains 36 CSV reports. Publication includes verification of the uploaded bytes, a collection manifest, and a completion marker after all required checks pass.

Google Drive access

The application requests access to files and folders selected by the operator, and files it creates, using Google's drive.file permission. It reads folder and account metadata to confirm the destination and authorized account.

Reports are organized into dated folders within the operator's three designated Google Drive export destinations. The Google approval screen lets the operator select the folders used for collection.
